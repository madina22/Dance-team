# Dance-team

A student-led dance community focused on creating an inclusive space for students to learn, practice, perform, and connect through dance.

## About the Project

The Dance Team is a student-led initiative created to bring together students who are interested in dance, regardless of their previous experience.

The project grew from my long-term involvement in dance and my experience organizing practices, performances, and community events. The main goal is to create a structured environment where students can develop their skills, work as a team, and participate in university activities.

Rather than focusing only on competitions, the team aims to make dance accessible as a form of self-expression, collaboration, and community building.

## Goals

* Create an active student dance community
* Provide regular opportunities to practice and improve
* Encourage students with different levels of experience to participate
* Prepare choreographies for university events
* Organize workshops and dance-related activities
* Develop teamwork, discipline, and confidence through performance

## Activities

The team can participate in and organize:

## Previous Experience

Before developing the university Dance Team, I gained experience in organizing and leading dance activities.

### Dance Studio

In Grade 9, I started a small dance studio in a rented basement after a friend who had previously organized the group left. The group initially consisted of three children and gradually grew to around 20 girls.

The studio was primarily community-oriented and focused on providing children with an opportunity to learn dance without making it a commercial activity.

### Performances & Competitions

The team participated in different competitions and events, including:

* **Crystal Droplet** — 2nd place
* **Just A Battle Showcase** — 1st place
* **Congress Hall Musical** — Top 3 among 21 teams
* **K-pop Party** — performance for approximately 1,000 students

The experience involved not only choreography but also organizing rehearsals, preparing performances, communicating with participants, and working with event organizers.

## Why This Project Matters

Dance can be more than a performance. It can become a way for students to find a community, express themselves, and develop skills outside the classroom.

The Dance Team is designed to create that community while giving students a practical opportunity to collaborate, lead, perform, and create something together.
